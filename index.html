11Questions

Good questions.

The things people usually ask before we start. Anything else, just ask.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
How much does a project cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, so you know the full price before anything starts. Because a small senior team does the work, a website revamp or a single dashboard usually costs far less than a traditional agency would charge.
How long does it take?
Most website revamps take 2 to 4 weeks. Dashboards, internal tools and MVPs usually take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope. You get a working preview every week, so you see progress long before launch.
Will a redesign hurt our Google rankings?
Not if it's done properly. We keep your important pages and URLs, set up redirects where anything moves, and fix the technical basics Google cares about. Most sites rank better after a rebuild because they load faster and are easier to read.
Can you add AI to the tools we already use?
Yes. We connect AI to things like your website forms, inbox, CRM, spreadsheets and booking system, so enquiries get answered, sorted and followed up automatically. We start with the one workflow that saves you the most time.
Can you deploy on our own infrastructure?
Yes. We can build with open-source speech and language models that run on your own or dedicated servers, so no third-party AI service is required. If you prefer cloud AI providers, we set them up on your own accounts and keep the system able to switch providers later.
Do we own the code and intellectual property?
Yes. Everything built specifically for your project is transferred to you with the source code and documentation, so your team or any developer can maintain it. We list any third-party components and their licences up front.
How do larger projects start?
With a paid technical audit. We review your platform and requirements, then deliver an architecture plan, a list of risks and a fixed quote for the build. You keep the audit whether or not we build it.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, happily. We can sign your NDA or send a mutual one before you share any details.
Do you work with businesses outside the UK?
Yes. We're a UK-registered company working remotely with clients in the UK, US, Australia and Asia, with calls scheduled in your time zone.
What happens after launch?
You own everything: the code, the design files and the accounts. We hand it over with a walkthrough, and if you want us to stay on for updates, fixes and new features, we offer simple monthly support.
