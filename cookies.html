What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Some are needed for a site to work, and others help the site owner understand how it is used.

The cookies we use

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not set any cookies of our own. The cookies on this site come from Google Analytics, which helps us understand how visitors use the site.

NameSet byPurposeExpires
_gaGoogle AnalyticsTells visits from different browsers apart2 years
_ga_KPXN9398W1Google AnalyticsKeeps track of your current visit2 years

Our contact and quote forms send your message to Formspree when you press send. They do not set cookies on this site.

The PolyMonit case study includes a YouTube video using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may store information on your device when you play the video.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies will not stop this website from working. You can also stop Google Analytics collecting data about your visits with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

More information

For more about how we handle personal information, see our privacy notice. If you have questions, email support@qoreonlabs.com.
