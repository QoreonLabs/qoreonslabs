05Questions

Web app questions.

What people usually ask before we build a booking or ordering app.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
Will it work with our current website?
Yes. We can add booking or ordering to the site you already have, or build it as part of a new site. Either way, it looks and feels like your brand, not a third-party widget.
Why not use Calendly, Fresha or a Shopify app?
If one of those fits how you work, use it, and we'll tell you so on the first call. A custom app makes sense when you need your own rules, like deposits for new customers only, several locations, staff skills or custom pricing, or when monthly fees keep rising as you grow.
Can customers pay online?
Yes. We use Stripe for deposits, full payments, pay-later invoices and refunds. Payments go straight to your own Stripe account, and card details never touch your servers.
How much does a web app cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call. The main things that drive cost are the number of screens, payment rules and integrations with your other systems.
How long does it take?
Most business web apps take 3 to 8 weeks. You get a working preview every week, so you can try the booking flow yourself long before launch.
What about our customers' data?
We store data in the UK or EU, give staff access by role and keep everything in accounts that you own. We'll also help you update your privacy notice to cover the new app.
