05Questions

Automation questions.

What people usually ask before automating their enquiries and follow-ups.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
Which tools can it work with?
Most of the ones you already use: website forms, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Business, Instagram, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Google Calendar, booking systems and Slack. If a tool has an API or sends email, we can usually connect it.
Will it sound like a robot?
No. We write the AI's instructions from your real replies, prices and policies, and test it on past enquiries until it sounds like your team. You approve the tone before anything goes live.
What if the AI gets something wrong?
It only answers from information you've given it, and it hands over to a person whenever a question is unusual, sensitive or a complaint. Every conversation is logged, so you can check and correct it.
How much does it cost?
Every project gets a fixed build quote after a free 15-minute call. Running costs, mostly AI usage and any tool subscriptions, are usually small and we estimate them up front.
How long does it take?
A single automation, such as enquiry replies and booking, usually takes 2 to 6 weeks to build, test on real examples and switch on.
What about our customers' data?
We only send the AI what it needs to reply, use providers that don't train on your data, and keep records in your own systems. For sensitive sectors like healthcare, we keep personal details out of the AI step wherever possible.
