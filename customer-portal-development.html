05Questions

Portal questions.

What people usually ask before we build a portal for their customers.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
Can it connect to our accounting or order system?
Usually, yes. We connect to tools like Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Shopify, HubSpot and most systems with an API, so orders and invoices appear in the portal without anyone retyping them.
Will our customers actually log in?
They will if it saves them time. We keep sign-in simple, often with a one-click email link instead of a password, and we make the first screen answer the question they usually email you about.
Can each customer only see their own data?
Yes. Every customer account is kept separate, and inside each account you can give people different roles, such as an owner, a buyer with a spending limit or a read-only viewer.
How much does a customer portal cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call. Cost depends mostly on how many systems we connect to and how many features customers need on day one.
How long does it take?
Most customer portals take 4 to 8 weeks. We often launch with a small group of friendly customers first, then roll it out to everyone once the feedback is in.
Can it use our brand and our own web address?
Yes. The portal uses your logo, colours and a web address like portal.yourcompany.com, so customers see it as part of your business, not a third-party tool.
