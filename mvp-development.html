05Questions

MVP questions.

What founders usually ask before we start an MVP.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
What should be in our MVP?
Only what's needed to test your biggest assumption with real users. On the first call we help you sort the feature list into now, next and later. Most ideas need far less than people expect to get a meaningful answer.
iPhone, Android or web?
It depends where your users are. We often start with iPhone in SwiftUI or a web app, because that is the fastest way to learn. If you need iPhone and Android from day one, we build both at once with React Native.
How much does an MVP cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, so you know the full price before anything starts. Keeping the scope tight is the best way to keep the cost down, and we will help you do that.
How long does it take?
Most MVPs take 3 to 8 weeks to a first version real users can try. You get a working build every week, on TestFlight for apps or a preview link for web, so you can show it to people as it grows.
Can you help with App Store submission?
Yes. We set up App Store Connect, TestFlight and the store listing, handle the review process and fix anything Apple flags. Your app is published under your own developer account.
What happens after the MVP?
You own the code and every account. If it works, we can keep building with you, or hand it over to your own team. Because we build the foundations properly, version two builds on version one instead of starting again.
