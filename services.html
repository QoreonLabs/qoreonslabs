05Questions

Good questions.

What people usually ask when they are not sure which service they need.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
We're not sure which service we need. Can you help?
Yes, that's what the free 15-minute call is for. Tell us what's slowing you down or what you want to launch, and we'll suggest the simplest thing that would fix it, even if that's not a build at all.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes. Most of our clients are small and growing businesses, from clinics and cafes to suppliers and startups. We keep projects focused, so they are worth it at that size.
Do you give fixed prices?
Yes. Every project gets a fixed quote after the first call, split into milestones. You only pay as each one is delivered, and you know the full price before anything starts.
Can you work with our existing website or software?
Usually, yes. We can improve or extend what you already have, connect to the tools you use, or plan a careful move to something new without losing your data or Google rankings.
Do you work with businesses outside the UK?
Yes. We're a UK-registered company working remotely with clients in the UK, US, Australia and Asia, with calls scheduled in your time zone.
What happens after launch?
You own everything: the code, the design files and the accounts. We hand it over with a walkthrough, and offer simple monthly support for updates, fixes and new features if you want it.
