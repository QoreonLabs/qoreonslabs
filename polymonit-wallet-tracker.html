02What we built

06Questions

PolyMonit questions.

Common questions about the product and this case study.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
What is PolyMonit?
PolyMonit is a wallet tracking and market intelligence tool for Polymarket. It follows large and successful wallets, shows their activity in a live feed and sends alerts when they trade.
Who built it?
Qoreon Labs designed, built and runs PolyMonit end to end, from the data pipeline to the web app, alerts, accounts and subscriptions.
Is PolyMonit financial advice?
No. PolyMonit shows public on-chain activity to help people research markets. It does not give financial advice or recommendations, and traders make their own decisions.
Can you build something like this for us?
Yes. If you have an idea for a data product, a dashboard or a subscription app, we can help you scope, build and launch it. See our SaaS development page or get in touch below.
