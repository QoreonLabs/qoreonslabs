05Questions

Internal tool questions.

What people usually ask before we replace a spreadsheet or a manual process.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
Can you move our existing spreadsheets across?
Yes. We clean up and import your current data as part of the build, so nobody starts from a blank screen. We check the import with you before launch and keep the old files as a backup.
Why not use Airtable, Notion or Retool?
Sometimes those are the right answer, and we'll say so on the first call. A custom tool makes sense when you need proper approval rules, per-person access, an audit trail, or when per-seat pricing gets expensive as your team grows.
How much does an internal tool cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, so you know the full price before anything starts. Cost depends mostly on how many screens, roles and integrations you need.
How long does it take?
Most internal tools take 3 to 8 weeks. You get a working preview every week, so your team can try it and give feedback long before launch.
Will my team actually use it?
That's the point of building it around how they already work. We keep screens simple, make it work on phones, and run a short training session at launch. Most teams need very little help after that.
Who owns the tool and the data?
You do. The code, the database and the hosting accounts are yours. We offer simple monthly support for changes and new features, or we hand it over to your own developer with a walkthrough.
