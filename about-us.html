01Our story

Built by people who build things.

We started as hands-on developers and still are. Everyone you talk to at Qoreon Labs is someone who builds.

Qoreon Labs began as a small development practice, building websites, apps and software for clients around the world. Over 100 delivered orders and a 5.0 rating from 69 reviews later, we became a UK limited company with a simple aim: give growing businesses the kind of senior, reliable product team that is usually only available to much bigger companies.

We also build products of our own. PolyMonit, our live data and alerts product, has paying subscribers in more than 20 countries. Running it every day keeps us honest about what it takes to launch software and keep it working long after launch day.

Who you work with. A small, senior team. The people on your first call are the people who plan, design and build your project, so nothing gets lost in a handover between sales and delivery.

Where we work. We are registered in London and work remotely with clients in the UK, US, Australia and Asia, with calls scheduled in your time zone.

What we focus on. Practical software for growing businesses: the website your customers see, the tools your team uses and the automations that join them up.

06Questions

About us.

Common questions about who we are and how we work.

Still unsure?Email support@qoreonlabs.com
Where is Qoreon Labs based?
Qoreon Labs Ltd is registered in England and Wales, with a registered office at 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. We work remotely with clients in the UK, US, Australia and Asia.
Who will I actually work with?
A small, senior team. The people on your first call are the people who plan and build your project, and you will have a direct line to them throughout.
Do you work with startups or established businesses?
Both. Most of our clients are growing businesses, from clinics and suppliers to agencies and early-stage founders. What they have in common is that they want something practical, built well.
What is PolyMonit?
PolyMonit is our own live product, a wallet tracking and alerts platform for Polymarket traders. We designed, built and run it end to end, and it has paying subscribers in more than 20 countries.
What is RedShield?
RedShield is our sister platform for testing AI features such as chatbots and AI agents. It checks for prompt injection, data leaks and unsafe answers. This site focuses on building products, and RedShield focuses on testing them.
How do I start a project?
Send us a few lines using the form below or email support@qoreonlabs.com. We reply within one business day and suggest a free 15-minute call to scope it, followed by a fixed quote and plan within 48 hours.
